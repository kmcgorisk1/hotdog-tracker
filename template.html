How the odds work

Every morning this page pulls the standings and schedule from ESPN and plays out the rest of the regular season 20,000 times. Each team's weekly score is based on what it has scored so far, with the last four weeks counting double, pulled slightly toward the league average because fantasy scoring is so random. During a live week, ESPN's live projections are used for that week.

The worst record after Week 14 eats the hot dogs. A tie goes to head-to-head record among the tied teams, then to fewest total points. A team's percentage is how often it finished last across all those simulated seasons. "Mathematically safe" means a team can't finish last even if it loses every remaining game.

When you pick winners, your browser re-runs 10,000 seasons with those results locked in. Nothing you pick changes the page for anyone else unless you send them your link.
